Halloween in the Coachella Valley: quick answers

What free Halloween events are there in the Coachella Valley?

Most of them are free: the Coachella Halloween Carnival (Oct 28), Halloween at VillageFest in Palm Springs and Hocus Pocus at Indio's Center Stage (Oct 29), the Trick or Treat Trail Ride in Cathedral City and the Desert Highland Halloween Carnival & Haunted House in Palm Springs (Oct 30), and the Thousand Palms Halloween Spooktacular (Oct 31). The Arenas Road parties are free too, with a suggested $10 donation.

Where is the big Halloween party in Palm Springs?

Halloween Palm Springs takes over the Arenas District downtown on Friday, Oct 30 and Saturday, Oct 31, 4 to 10 PM both nights, with DJs and a costume contest. Admission is free, with a suggested $10 donation to LGBTQ charities.

Is there a haunted house in the Coachella Valley this year?

Yes. Cabot's Pueblo Museum in Desert Hot Springs runs a haunted house Oct 30 and 31 from 4 to 9 PM ($10 a person or $20 for four). The James O. Jessie Desert Highland Unity Center in Palm Springs has a free carnival and haunted house on Oct 30 from 4 to 7 PM.

What can kids do on Halloween morning?

Howl-O-Ween at The Living Desert in Palm Desert runs 9 AM to 1 PM on Oct 31, with trick-or-treat stations included with zoo admission. The Palm Springs Air Museum's chili tasting and car show starts at 10 AM, and kids 12 and under get in free in costume.

Where can I celebrate Día de los Muertos?

On Nov 1 there's a free Día de los Muertos Family Day at the Coachella Valley History Museum in Indio and a brunch at the Demuth Community Center in Palm Springs. Sunnylands in Rancho Mirage has its Days of Los Muertos Nov 4 to 8, and Run with Los Muertos fills Old Town Coachella on Nov 7.

Times and details come from each organizer and local news listings, and they can change, so check the event's own page before you go. Hosting a Halloween event that isn't here? Add it free.

Questions

Pricing questions

What is the cheapest way to get started?

A Basic listing in our directory is free. On the 9 x 12 postcard, a Half ad with a QR code is $175 per mailing to nearly 5,000 homes, about 3.5 cents a home. Design, printing and postage are included.

Is there a contract?

No. You book one mailing at a time. Come back for the next card if it works for you.

What does one business per category mean?

When you book, your category is locked on that card. If you are the dentist on the El Paseo card, no other dentist can buy a spot on it.

What do I need to send?

Your logo, your offer and your phone number. We design the ad, and you approve it before anything prints.

When do I pay?

Your choice. Pay online when you book, and your spot is locked in right away. Or hold your spot and we send an invoice, and it is locked in once it is paid.

What if a card doesn’t mail on its date?

Mail dates are tentative until we mark a card confirmed on the site. If a card isn’t ready by its artwork deadline, it moves to the next mailing date. Your spot, your category and your payment carry over, or we refund you if you would rather not wait.

What is the deadline to get on the next mailing?

Artwork is usually due 7 days before the mail date. Each card shows its exact deadline on the mailing calendar and at checkout, and that date is the one that counts. Book early to secure your spot, because exclusive categories fill up fast.

Do you offer discounts for multiple mailings?

Yes, the Season Pass. Book three mailings in a row (Jan 21, Feb 18 and Mar 18) with the same spot and your category locked on all three, and you get $50 off every spot on every mailing: a Full is $900 for all three (instead of $1,050) and a Half is $375 (instead of $525). Want more homes on one date instead? The Two-route deal puts the same ad on two routes that mail the same day (about 10,000 homes): a Full is $600 for both (instead of $700) and a Half is $300 for both (instead of $350). It can’t be combined with the Season Pass.

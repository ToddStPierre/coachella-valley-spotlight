Pricing questions

What is the cheapest way to get started?

A Basic listing in our directory is free. On the postcard, a single spot is $300 per mailing to about 5,000 homes, with design, printing and postage included.

Is there a contract?

No. You book one mailing at a time. Come back for the next card if it works for you.

What does one business per category mean?

When you book, your category is locked on that card. If you are the dentist on the El Paseo card, no other dentist can buy a spot on it.

What do I need to send?

Your logo, your offer and your phone number. We design the ad, and you approve it before anything prints.

When do I pay?

After your ad is approved, we send an invoice. Your spot is locked in once it is paid.

What if a card doesn’t reach 8 spots sold?

A card mails once at least 8 of its 16 spots are sold. If it has fewer by the artwork deadline, it moves to the next mailing date. Your spot, your category and your payment carry over, or we refund you if you would rather not wait.

Questions

Questions owners ask before they book

How big is my ad?

From the Jan 21 mailing on (and on the Indio and La Quinta cards from Dec 17), the card is a big 9 x 12 postcard with two ad sizes. A Full ad is 4.4 by 2.6 inches, big enough for a photo, your logo, your offer, your phone number and a QR code. A Half ad (2.1 by 2.6 inches) fits your logo, offer, phone and a QR code. You can also join spots side by side for a bigger ad, like two Fulls as an 8.8 by 2.6 inch banner.

Which towns do you mail?

Seven cards: El Paseo (92260) and Sun City (92211) in Palm Desert, north Indio (92203), two in La Quinta (92253): La Quinta and PGA West, Rancho Mirage (92270) and Indian Wells (92210). El Paseo mails Nov 27 and Sun City Dec 17, 2026, then all seven mail Jan 21, Feb 18 and Mar 18, 2027. See every card on the coverage map.

Who gets the card?

Every home on the card you pick, delivered by the U.S. Postal Service. That is 4,968 homes on El Paseo, 4,829 on Sun City, 4,791 on Indio, 4,916 on La Quinta, 4,677 on PGA West, 4,976 on Rancho Mirage and 4,925 on Indian Wells, and every home on the route gets one, not a rented list.

Can my competitor be on the same card?

No. We take one business per category per card. If you are the plumber on the El Paseo card, you are the only plumber on it. If it ever happens, that mailing is free.

I don’t have an ad. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Design is included. Send us your logo, your offer and your phone number, we lay it out, and you approve it before anything prints.

What does the price cover?

Your ad, the design, printing, postage and QR code tracking (Small and up). On the 9 x 12 card a Half is $175 and a Full is $350 per mailing, and a bigger ad is the price of the spots it joins. There is no setup fee and no contract to sign. You also get a free Premium listing on this site while your ad runs.

When does it mail, and how do I pay?

The El Paseo card mails Friday, November 27, 2026. The Sun City card is tentatively set to mail Thursday, December 17, 2026, with artwork due Monday, December 7. Dates stay tentative until we mark a card confirmed on the site. If a card isn’t ready by its artwork deadline, it moves to the next mailing date. Your spot, your category and your payment carry over to the new date, or we refund you if you would rather not wait. Every upcoming card is on the mailing calendar. Every ad and Season Pass is paid in full, with no monthly billing. Pay online when you book and your spot is locked in right away, or hold it and we send you an invoice.

Can I stop a Season Pass early?

Yes. Stop after any card and tell us. We refund what you paid, minus the regular single-mailing price ($350 Full, $175 Half) for each card already sent. Example: a Full Season Pass is $900. Stop after the first card and you get $550 back.

What is the deadline to get on the next mailing?

Artwork is usually due 7 days before the mail date. Each card shows its exact deadline on the mailing calendar and at checkout, and that date is the one that counts. Book early to secure your spot, because exclusive categories fill up fast.

Do you offer discounts for multiple mailings?

Yes. Two ways. The Season Pass locks your spot on all three winter cards (Jan 21, Feb 18, Mar 18) with $50 off every spot on every mailing. The Two-route deal puts the same ad on two routes that mail the same day, about 10,000 homes, with $50 off each Full spot and $25 off each Half spot: $600 for two Fulls, $300 for two Halves. The two can't be combined.

What’s the difference between a category sponsor and a Premium listing?

A Premium listing makes your own listing better: photos, hours, your offer and monthly stats, and it sits above free listings. A category sponsor puts your ad at the top of every page in your whole category, above everyone, and only one business can have it. Sponsors get Premium included.

Can I do more than one?

Yes, and it costs less together. Every postcard ad comes with a free Premium listing while it runs, and postcard advertisers can add the category sponsor spot for $99 a month instead of $149.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Postcard ads are paid in full for each mailing, or up front for a Season Pass, which you can stop after any card. Premium and category sponsorship are month to month, and you can cancel anytime.

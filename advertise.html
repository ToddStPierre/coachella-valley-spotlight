Questions

Questions owners ask before they book

How big is my ad?

Each spot is 2.7 by 2.5 inches on an oversized 11 by 6 inch postcard. That is big enough for your logo, your offer, your phone number and a QR code.

Who gets the card?

About 5,000 households on the route you pick, delivered by the U.S. Postal Service. Every home on the route gets one, not a rented list.

Can my competitor be on the same card?

No. We take one business per category per card. If you are the plumber on the El Paseo card, you are the only plumber on it.

I don’t have an ad. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Design is included. Send us your logo, your offer and your phone number, we lay it out, and you approve it before anything prints.

What does the $300 cover?

Your spot, the ad design, printing and postage. There is no setup fee and no contract to sign. You also get a free Premium listing on this site while your ad runs.

When does it mail, and how do I pay?

We give you the mailing date when you reserve. We send an invoice, and your spot is locked in once it is paid.

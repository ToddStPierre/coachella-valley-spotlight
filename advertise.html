Questions

Questions owners ask before they book

How big is my ad?

One spot is 2.7 by 2.6 inches on an oversized 11 by 6 inch postcard, big enough for your logo, your offer, your phone number and a QR code. Want more room? Take 2 spots side by side (5.4 by 2.6 inches), a 2 by 2 block of 4 (5.4 by 5.2 inches), or a whole side of the card.

Who gets the card?

Every home on the route you pick: 4,972 on El Paseo and 4,826 on Sun City Palm Desert, delivered by the U.S. Postal Service. Every home on the route gets one, not a rented list.

Can my competitor be on the same card?

No. We take one business per category per card. If you are the plumber on the El Paseo card, you are the only plumber on it.

I don’t have an ad. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Design is included. Send us your logo, your offer and your phone number, we lay it out, and you approve it before anything prints.

What does the $300 cover?

Your spot, the ad design, printing, postage and QR code tracking. Bigger ads cost less per spot: 2 spots are $550, 4 spots are $1,000 and a full side is $2,000. There is no setup fee and no contract to sign. You also get a free Premium listing on this site while your ad runs.

When does it mail, and how do I pay?

The next cards are tentatively set to mail Friday, November 27, 2026, with artwork tentatively due Monday, November 16. A card mails once at least 8 of its 16 spots are sold. If a card has fewer than 8 spots sold by its artwork deadline, it moves to the next mailing date. Your spot, your category and your payment carry over to the new date, or we refund you if you would rather not wait. Every upcoming card is on the mailing calendar. Pay online when you book and your spot is locked in right away, or hold it and we send you an invoice.

What is the deadline to get on the next mailing?

Artwork is usually due 7 days before the mail date. Each card shows its exact deadline on the mailing calendar and at checkout, and that date is the one that counts. Book early to secure your spot, because exclusive categories fill up fast.

Do you offer discounts for multiple mailings?

Yes. We offer package pricing for businesses that commit to multiple mailings, and for bundling neighboring zones. Contact us for details.

What’s the difference between a category sponsor and a Premium listing?

A Premium listing makes your own listing better: photos, hours, your offer and monthly stats, and it sits above free listings. A category sponsor puts your ad at the top of every page in your whole category, above everyone, and only one business can have it. Sponsors get Premium included.

Can I do more than one?

Yes, and it costs less together. Every postcard ad comes with a free Premium listing while it runs, and postcard advertisers can add the category sponsor spot for $99 a month instead of $149.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Postcard spots are paid per mailing. Premium and category sponsorship are month to month, and you can cancel anytime.

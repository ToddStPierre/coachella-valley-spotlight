Who’s behind Spotlight

Run by a neighbor in Palm Desert.

Not a national company, not a lead-selling network. One person who lives here, keeping one useful page for the whole valley.

Hi, I’m Todd St. Pierre.

I live in Palm Desert, and I run Coachella Valley Spotlight myself. I add the listings, check the events, choose which local newsrooms the headlines come from, and answer the phone when you call.

Why I built it

Every time I searched “things to do near me,” most of what came back was in Palm Springs. I don’t live in Palm Springs. The rest of the valley’s businesses, events and places get buried. I wanted one place for the whole valley, where the businesses are real, the phone numbers ring someone local, and the events and deals are actually happening this week.

How Spotlight pays for itself

Listing a business here is free, and so is everything you read. The site is paid for by the Spotlight postcard, a shared card mailed to about 5,000 homes per route. Local businesses buy a spot on it. Those businesses are marked Sponsor wherever they appear on this site, so you always know which listings are paid. Everyone else is listed for free, and no business can pay to change what’s written about another.

One more thing, in plain view: I’m also a licensed California real estate agent (DRE #02100619) with All in One Realty & Mortgage, one of the businesses on the card.

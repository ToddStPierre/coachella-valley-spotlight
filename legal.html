Privacy, terms and disclaimer

Last updated October 19, 2026.

Privacy

When you fill out a form on this site, we collect what you type in (like your name, email, phone and business details) so we can reply, post your listing or set up your ad. Forms are delivered by a third-party form service. We do not sell your information.

To show local businesses how their listing is doing, the site counts when a business listing is seen, when its profile is opened, and when someone taps its phone number or website link. These are anonymous totals per business. We do not use cookies for this and do not record who you are.

To ask us to update or delete what you sent, call or text (760) 422-5475.

Terms

Listings, events, deals and news on this site are shared for general information. Businesses are responsible for the accuracy of their own offers, prices and hours. We may edit or decline any submission.

Postcard ad spots are confirmed once invoiced and paid. Mailing dates and route counts are approximate and set at the time of booking.

Disclaimer

Home counts are approximate and based on postal route data. We do not guarantee any specific number of calls, sales or results from advertising. Listing a business here is not an endorsement. This site is independent and is not affiliated with any city, festival or event it mentions.

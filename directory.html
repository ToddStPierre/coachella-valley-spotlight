About the directory

Coachella Valley Spotlight is a free directory of local businesses across the desert, from Palm Springs and Cathedral City to Rancho Mirage, Palm Desert, Indian Wells, La Quinta, Indio, Coachella and Desert Hot Springs. It is built for people who would rather call a local business than a national call center: plumbers, roofers, air conditioning and pest control companies, flooring and wallpaper pros, clinics and acupuncturists, and the restaurants your neighbors keep going back to.

Every business has its own page with its phone number, website and service area. Premium listings also show hours, photos and current offers. Many of the businesses here also advertise on the Spotlight postcard, which goes to about 5,000 homes per route in Palm Desert. Owners can claim their listing for free and keep it up to date. If you know a local business that should be here, send it in and we will add it at no cost.

Things to do in the Coachella Valley

This calendar collects things to do across the Coachella Valley: concerts at Acrisure Arena and the McCallum Theatre, Coachella Valley Firebirds hockey, Greater Palm Springs Pride, street fairs, Dia de los Muertos celebrations, community events at Sunnylands and more. Events are sorted by date, and each one has its own page with the time, venue, city and a link to the organizer's official page for tickets and details.

Past events drop off the calendar on their own, so what you see is always coming up. The calendar covers Palm Springs, Cathedral City, Rancho Mirage, Palm Desert, Indian Wells, La Quinta, Indio, Coachella, Thousand Palms and Desert Hot Springs. Hosting a show, a market, a fundraiser or a class? Send it in. Listing an event is free. Details can change, so check with the organizer before you head out.

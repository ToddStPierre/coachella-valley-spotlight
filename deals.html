About these deals

These are current deals and specials from local Coachella Valley businesses: restaurant offers, discounts on home services like pest control, and other savings from shops and service pros in Palm Desert and around the valley. Each deal links to the business's own page, where you will find the phone number, website and the fine print.

Deals come straight from the businesses, many of them from the ads they run on the Spotlight postcard that is mailed to Palm Desert homes. Expired deals drop off on their own, so everything you see here is good today. Mention Coachella Valley Spotlight when you call or visit so the business knows where you found them.
